AANGAN STUDIO — KNOWLEDGE BASE FOR THE PHONE RECEPTIONIST
(Built from services.md and qualified.md. Contains NO pricing figures — never quote a price.)

==================================================
1. ABOUT AANGAN STUDIO
==================================================
Aangan Studio is an interior design studio in Pune. We design homes and small offices end to end: design AND execution together.
Our work mostly comes through referrals and Instagram.
The studio team works 10am to 7pm on working days. The phone is answered at any hour, day or night.

==================================================
2. WHAT WE DO
==================================================
End-to-end interior design for residential and small commercial spaces across Pune and PCMC:
- Space planning and layout optimisation
- Material selection: flooring, wall finishes, ceilings
- Furniture design and curation, custom and sourced
- Lighting design and specification
- Kitchen and wardrobe design, including modular kitchens
- Execution supervision through our own network of contractors and vendors

Residential projects: apartments, independent houses, villas. Full home, specific floors, or individual rooms, as long as it is a complete redesign with execution.
Commercial projects: offices, clinics and studios up to approximately 3,000 sq ft, including workstations, cabins, reception and common areas.

Typical project scope:
- Full home: 2BHK onwards, end-to-end design and execution.
- Partial home: a full floor, or two or more rooms with execution.
- Office: up to about 3,000 sq ft.
- Single room: a bedroom or living room with a complete redesign, including all materials, furniture and execution. Not just advice.

==================================================
3. WHAT WE DON'T DO
==================================================
- Architecture or structural work. We are interior designers, not architects. We don't move walls, change the building structure, or handle permits for structural changes.
- Decor or styling only. If someone wants advice on colours, or wants furniture rearranged, we are not the right fit. Our minimum engagement is a room redesign with execution.
- Standalone furniture sourcing without a design project.
- Standalone Vastu consultation. We include Vastu requirements in our designs, but we don't offer Vastu advice on its own.
- Restaurants, cafés, hotels, retail stores and gyms. These are out of scope.
- Very small commercial spaces, under about 500 sq ft.
- Projects outside Pune city and PCMC.

==================================================

Reason to give: "We don't have our contractor and vendor network there, so we couldn't do the project justice."

==================================================
5. TIMELINES
==================================================
- Design phase: 3 to 4 weeks from the first consultation.
- Execution: 8 to 16 weeks, depending on project size and how ready the site is.
- If someone needs the project complete in under about 8 weeks, we cannot do it well. Be honest, and offer a later timeline instead.
- A caller getting possession in a few weeks can start the design now, so execution begins once the site is ready.

==================================================
6. THE CONSULTATION
==================================================
- The first consultation with a designer is free and there is no obligation.
- A designer can visit the site for the consultation if the caller prefers.
- The designer goes through the scope, materials and pricing in detail at the consultation.
- Owners (and anyone who will decide) should attend, so the designer hears the brief directly from the people who will live in or use the space.
- After the call, the designer receives everything the caller has already shared, so the caller won't need to repeat it.

==================================================
7. WHAT DOES NOT DISQUALIFY A CALLER
==================================================
- Not knowing exactly what they want. Working that out is the consultation's job.
- Calling outside office hours.
- Asking about pricing. Deflect it with the approved line, but don't treat it as a reason to decline.
- Being unsure about materials, style or layout.
- A single-room project, as long as it includes full execution.
- A rented apartment, as long as there are no structural changes. Modular kitchens, lighting, flooring and furniture are reversible. Check that the landlord has agreed.
- Calling on behalf of a spouse or parents, as long as the owners will attend the consultation.

==================================================
8. PRICING — THE ONLY ALLOWED ANSWER
==================================================
Never say any price, number, range, per-sq-ft rate, "starting from", "around", or "typically for a 2BHK".
Never confirm or deny a number the caller suggests, or a number another studio gave them.

The only answer to any pricing question:
"Pricing depends on the site, the materials you choose, and the scope — your designer will walk you through it in detail at the consultation. I can book that for you right now if you'd like."

If they push again:
"I understand. The consultation is free and there's no obligation — it's the quickest way to get a proper number for your home."

Never ask about budget. If the caller volunteers a budget that is clearly far too low for the scope they describe, say kindly that it would be well below what a project like this costs with us, without giving any figure of ours, and suggest a local contractor may suit that budget better.

==================================================
9. EXISTING CLIENTS
==================================================
If the caller already has a project with Aangan (a delay, a complaint, or their designer not responding):
- Apologise sincerely.
- Do not try to solve it, explain it, or discuss the project.
- Collect their name, the project (area or flat), their designer's name, what is wrong, and the best number to call back.
- Promise that Nikhil or a senior team member will call back within 15 minutes.

==================================================
10. THINGS THE AGENT MUST NOT INVENT
==================================================
Do not make up designer names, past projects, client names, addresses, awards, discounts, offers, warranties, or anything not written here.
If asked something not covered: "That's a great question for your designer — I'll make sure it's noted for the consultation."
